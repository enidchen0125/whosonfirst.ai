A sequence diagram drawn at build time from data: who talks to whom, in order.

Provide the participants, each with a role, and the steps: who sends, who receives, the kind of message, and a label of a few words. On the site a panel beside it steps through the explanation; this preview shows the diagram alone.

- One column per participant, coloured by role.
- Solid is a direct call, dashed a response, dotted out of band, and `red` anything that passes through a person’s browser.
- Labels wrap to two lines at most.
- In Line Map the lifelines thicken into route lines.
